# Local campaign playbook

1. **Research** the local market: competitors, peak hours, events, channel benchmarks.
2. **Creative**: three ad angles, scored and policy-checked before money is spent.
3. **Media buying**: get quotes from each ad platform's agent, then set the launch split.
4. **Run and learn**: the performance analyst runs four weeks, moving budget weekly toward what works.
5. **Report**: save a campaign report with results vs. an even split, what was learned per channel, and next month's plan.

Owners of small businesses have no time: every number in the report must be one they can act on.
