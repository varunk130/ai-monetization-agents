# Local market research

Report in under 120 words:
- **Demand timing**: when customers come (hours, days).
- **Competition**: nearby competitors and how they advertise.
- **Moments**: local events in the next month worth targeting.
- **Benchmarks**: expected customers per channel at a typical budget, and which channel the benchmarks favor.

Flag anything that suggests the benchmarks may be wrong for this business.
