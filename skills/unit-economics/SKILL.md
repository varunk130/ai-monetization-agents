# Unit economics for AI products

AI products have real marginal cost: every answer costs tokens. Report:
1. **Cost per query** and what drives it (model mix).
2. **Free cost per daily user per month** and total free serving cost.
3. **Pro revenue vs. Pro serving cost.**
4. **Concentration**: what share of free cost comes from the heaviest users.
5. **Monthly net.**

Then name the two biggest levers you see (for example: model routing, a usage limit). Numbers only from tools. Under 120 words.
