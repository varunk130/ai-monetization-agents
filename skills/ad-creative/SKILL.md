# Ad creative

1. Generate three angles: **Offer**, **Quality**, **Convenience**.
2. Score each. Good local ads have a concrete offer, say where you are, fit the headline on one line, give a clear next step, and use a specific number.
3. Run the policy check. Fix anything flagged (superlatives like "best", guarantees, all caps, repeated punctuation) before it ships.
4. Return the three ads with scores and the one to lead with.
