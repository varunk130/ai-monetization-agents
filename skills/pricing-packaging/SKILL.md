# Pricing and packaging

## Levers, in order of user impact (lowest first)
1. **Model routing**: send routine free queries to a smaller model. Invisible to most users.
2. **Daily limit on free usage**: caps heavy users. Set it above what most free users need, so it touches a minority.
3. **Price change**: affects every subscriber; model elasticity explicitly.

## Method
Simulate a baseline plus 3 to 4 options. Compare monthly net, share of free users who hit the limit, and free users lost. Recommend the option with the best net that keeps users hitting the limit under 25%. Show the limit before users reach it; never as a surprise.
