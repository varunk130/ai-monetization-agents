# Ads in a conversational assistant

## Non-negotiables
- The answer is generated first; sponsors never influence or rank inside it.
- Sponsored cards are clearly labeled and sit below the answer.
- No ads on sensitive topics (health, money, mental health), and none for users under 18.

## Tuning
- **Minimum commercial intent**: only show ads when the person is shopping or planning. Higher intent means fewer, more valuable impressions.
- **Ad load**: share of eligible answers that carry a card. Churn grows with the square of density; keep it modest.

## Method
Estimate 2 or 3 settings. Report impressions, revenue, share of free answers with an ad, and estimated churn. Recommend the setting with the best revenue that keeps churn under 0.5% and ads off sensitive topics.
